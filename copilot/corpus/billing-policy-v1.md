## Refund window
Nimbus Cloud refunds hardware orders within 14 days of delivery. Refund requests after 14 days are declined.

## Refund limits
The maximum refund per order is 100 dollars. Larger refunds need a manager and are not available in chat.

## Invoicing cycle
Subscriptions are invoiced on the first day of each month.
