## Refund window
Nimbus Cloud refunds hardware orders within 30 days of delivery. Refund requests made after 30 days of delivery are declined.

## Refund eligibility
Only delivered orders can be refunded. Each order can be refunded once, and the refund amount can never exceed the order total.

## Refund limits
The maximum refund per request is 500 dollars. Refunds above this limit are escalated to a human agent.

## Invoicing cycle
Subscriptions are invoiced on the first day of each month and payment is due within 15 days.
